Top of the SERP Studio

Top of the SERP Studio is a private production tool used by the owner of the Top of the SERP YouTube channel, which publishes explainer videos about SEO and AI search for small businesses. The videos use animated graphics and an AI narration voice.

The tool runs on the owner's own computer. It helps research, plan and render videos, uploads them to that one channel, and reads that channel's statistics to decide what to make next. It is not offered to other users or other channels.
