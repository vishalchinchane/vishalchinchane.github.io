Neeti Katha Studio

Neeti Katha Studio is a private production tool used by the owner of the Neeti Katha Kids YouTube channel, which publishes Marathi moral stories for children made with AI tools.

The tool runs on the owner's own computer. It helps plan and render videos, uploads them to that one channel, and reads that channel's statistics to decide what to make next. It is not offered to other users or other channels.
