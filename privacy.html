Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Neeti Katha Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the Neeti Katha Kids YouTube channel, on their own computer. It has no other users and no website accounts.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

  • The signed-in channel's public details, its list of uploads and their statistics.
  • The channel's YouTube Analytics reports, which are aggregated and do not identify viewers.
  • Permission to upload videos to that channel.
  • Public YouTube search results, for topic research.

The tool does not collect personal information about viewers, including children. It does not read comments, and it does not use cookies or tracking.

Where data is kept and who receives it

The Google sign-in token and reports are stored only on the owner's computer. Channel statistics are sent to Anthropic's Claude service for analysis when the owner runs the tool. Story text is sent to Sarvam AI to generate narration. Data is not sold, or used for advertising.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Deleting the token and reports from the owner's computer removes the locally stored data.
